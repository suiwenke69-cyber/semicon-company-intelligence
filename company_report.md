## 1. Company Overview

- DUV is a lithography technology that uses deep ultraviolet (DUV) light. `[c_aa250aacbf]`
- EUV is a lithography technology that uses extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light with a wavelength of 13.5 nm. `[c_b62a1cbbf5]`
- EXE is ASML’s second TWINSCAN platform for EUV lithography, also referred to as EUV 0.55 NA or High NA EUV. `[c_1da53e6464]`
- A lithography technique uses a pool of ultrapure water between the lens and the wafer to increase the lens’s numerical aperture. `[c_30ac2fc644]`
- This improves both the resolution and depth of focus for the lithography system. `[c_3116eba0da]`
- Lithography, or photolithography, is the process in microchip manufacturing that uses light to pattern parts on a silicon wafer. `[c_4770156faa]`
- ASML’s first TWINSCAN platform for EUV lithography provides 13 nm resolution to support advanced Logic and Memory chip production. `[c_81d9d088e3]`
- TWINSCAN is ASML’s unique lithography system platform, with two complete wafer stages to allow one wafer to be mapped while another is being exposed, thereby enabling higher accuracy and throughput. `[c_5874805c84]`
