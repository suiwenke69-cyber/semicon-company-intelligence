## 1. Company Overview

- Applied Materials, Inc. is the leader in the materials engineering solutions used to produce virtually every semiconductor in the world. `[c_5bdff19d7d]`
- Our Semiconductor Systems segment designs, develops, manufactures and sells a wide range of equipment used to fabricate semiconductor chips, also referred to as integrated circuits (ICs). `[c_ee8bcbcd6b]`
- Our AGS segment provides services, spares and factory automation software to customer fabrication plants globally. `[c_5bdfc4b3cb]`
- Through October 26, 2025, our AGS segment also manufactured and sold 200 millimeter (200mm) and other equipment to customers globally that serve non-leading-edge markets. `[c_216c92ffe2]`
- Effective the first quarter of fiscal 2026, our 200mm equipment business will be moved to our Semiconductor Systems segment. `[c_4b1a341e16]`
- We provide equipment, services and software to the semiconductor and related industries. `[c_fd15c73527]`
- We operate in two reportable segments: Semiconductor Systems and Applied Global Services&#174; (AGS). `[c_b97ab8f5c2]`
- The Semiconductor Systems segment is comprised primarily of capital equipment used to fabricate semiconductor chips. `[c_4879d8cb23]`
