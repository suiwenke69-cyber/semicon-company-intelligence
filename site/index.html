Reading the reports

Claim tags

FACT stated directly in a cited filing.

ANALYSIS reasoned across two or more facts.

INFERENCE reasoned with an explicit chain — this includes all peer-filing evidence, since a competitor describing itself is not a fact about the subject company.

Honest limitations

An issuer's own annual report is often a poor source for competitor detail and product specifics. Applied Materials' Form 10-K contains the word “deposition” once and names no competitor at all.

Coverage tables report categories the sources did not support rather than filling them in.

No investment recommendation is made or implied.
